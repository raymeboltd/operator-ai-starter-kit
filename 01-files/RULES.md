# Rules

## Allowed without approval

- Read the files explicitly placed in this workspace.
- Summarize, compare, classify, and draft.
- Propose reversible changes with evidence.

## Approval required

- Publish, message, purchase, delete, or change a production system.
- Access a new data source or expose private information.
- Change an agreed account, audience, deadline, or final copy.

## Stop conditions

Stop and ask for a decision when:

- the requested outcome is ambiguous;
- sources conflict and no authority rule resolves them;
- the action is irreversible or externally visible;
- a required fact cannot be verified;
- the same workflow fails twice for the same reason.

## Evidence rule

Every factual claim carries a source. Every completed action carries a receipt from
the destination system. A confident summary is not a receipt.
