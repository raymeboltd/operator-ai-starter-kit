# Example: weekly operating review

## Outcome

A one-page decision brief with completed work, unresolved commitments, next-week
priorities, and the three decisions that need an owner.

## Inputs

- Calendar events from the last seven days.
- Meeting notes.
- Project status files.
- Open commitments.

## Process

1. Collect the inputs without changing them.
2. Deduplicate repeated work and commitments.
3. Separate facts, interpretations, and proposals.
4. Link every claim to its source.
5. Draft the brief.
6. Ask the owner to approve priorities and assignments.

## Receipt

The review is complete when the approved priorities exist in the real project system,
with an owner and date. The generated summary alone is not completion.
